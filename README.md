# 🌿 Friends Space

> A simple, private social space to track and share your daily **Move, Read, and Screen Time** with friends.

## ✨ What is Friends Space?

Friends Space helps you stay accountable with your friends while building better daily habits.

### 🏃 Move
Track your:
- Steps
- Distance
- Active time

### 📖 Read
Track your:
- Current book
- Author
- Reading progress
- Pages read

### 📱 Screen
Track your:
- Total daily screen time
- Change compared with yesterday

## 🔒 Privacy First

Your data belongs to you.

- Choose which activities you want to share.
- Only accepted friends can see your shared progress.
- Screen time is shared only as a daily total.
- Detailed app-by-app screen usage is never shared.
- Your private information stays private by default.

## 👥 Connect With Friends

- Find friends using their username.
- Send and accept friend requests.
- View your friends' shared progress.
- Control what information you share through privacy settings.
