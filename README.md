🌿 Friends Space

A simple, private social space to track and share your daily Move, Read, and Screen Time with friends.

What is Friends Space?

Friends Space helps you stay accountable with your friends while building healthier daily habits.

Track:

🏃 Move — steps, distance and active time
📖 Read — current book and reading progress
📱 Screen — daily total screen time and change from yesterday
🔒 Privacy First

Your data belongs to you.

Choose what you want to share with friends.
Only accepted friends can see your shared progress.
Screen time is shared only as a daily total, never as detailed app usage.
Your private information stays private.
👥 Connect With Friends
Find friends using their username.
Send and accept friend requests.
See your friends' shared daily progress.
Control what each friend can see through your privacy settings.
